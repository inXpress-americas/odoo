InXpress Shipping

Rates, dispatch, labels and tracking, inside Odoo

Quote a sales order against your InXpress carriers, book the shipment from the transfer, and get the label and tracking back without leaving Odoo.

What it does

Rates on the sales order

Rates come from your own InXpress account, so the prices shown are the prices you are contracted to. Odoo adds the cheapest one as a delivery line. Click the Compare Rates button instead and you get every service returned, with its name and transit time, and you pick.

Dispatch from the transfer

Booking happens on the delivery order, from the boxes actually packed. InXpress takes one set of package details per shipment, so the module sends the number of boxes, the shipment weight split evenly across them, and the dimensions of the largest box. If the boxes differ a lot in weight, Odoo logs a warning that the heaviest one may be under-rated.

Labels and tracking

The label is attached to the transfer when you dispatch, and the tracking number is written to the picking. The Tracking button on the transfer refreshes the shipment status before it opens the carrier page. To refresh status automatically every 4 hours, activate the scheduled action "InXpress: Sync Tracking" under Settings > Technical > Scheduled Actions. It is off when the module is installed.

Cancellation

Cancel a booked shipment from the transfer. The module voids it with the carrier and clears the tracking reference.

Parcel and freight

A carrier runs in parcel mode or in freight (LTL) mode. Freight quotes declare a handling unit, a pallet, crate or skid, with its own dimensions and a freight class. Accessorials cover residential delivery and a liftgate at pickup or at delivery.

Freight needs an InXpress API token carrying the freight:quote and freight:write scopes. A parcel-only token quotes parcel and nothing else.

Units are never assumed

The connector reads the weight, length and volume units set in your Odoo database and converts before sending. Kilograms go out with centimetres, pounds with inches. InXpress never receives a mixed pair, and nobody has to remember which database is set to which.

International shipments

A cross-border dispatch includes a customs declaration built from the goods actually picked, with HS code and country of origin read from the product form. Any product missing an HS code is named in the log by product and picking, so you find out from Odoo rather than from a shipment held at the border.

Setup

Create a delivery method, set its provider to InXpress, and enter your InXpress API URL and API token. An InXpress account is required. The module talks to InXpress only, never to carriers directly, so a rate that looks wrong is a question for InXpress support.

Rates are used as returned, without currency conversion. Your Odoo company currency must be the same as the currency of your InXpress account, otherwise delivery prices on orders will be wrong.
